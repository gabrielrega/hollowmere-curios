# Hollowmere Curios

A cozy low-poly fantasy shopkeeping game for mobile browsers, built with three.js.

Aunt Wren left you her curio shop at the crossroads, and a standing debt to the Merchants' Guild. Adventurers come in, take things off your shelves and bring them to the counter. You name the price.

## How to play

- **Haggle.** Every counter-offer is below what the customer will really pay. Push too far and they storm out, costing reputation.
- **Buy from sellers.** Goblin peddlers and broke adventurers will sell you goods cheaply.
- **Restock at night.** Pay the rising guild dues, buy wholesale stock, and build upgrades (extra shelves, sconces, an appraiser's loupe, a sign, a shop cat).
- **Read the rumors.** Tavern gossip moves tomorrow's demand more than tonight's wholesale prices, and effects fade over several days.
- **Win** the Guild Charter at 2,000 gold. Three nights in debt and the Guild takes the shop.

Progress saves in the browser's local storage each night.

## Running it

It's a single self-contained file. Open `index.html` in a browser, or enable GitHub Pages on this repo (Settings → Pages → deploy from `main`, root) to play it online. three.js r128 loads from cdnjs.
